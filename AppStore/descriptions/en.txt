# [description]
Animal Spin is a simple, gentle app made for the youngest kids. Tap an animal and your child hears its name spoken aloud, followed by a real recorded animal sound. That's the whole app. No menus to get lost in, no scores, no AI-generated content. Just tap, listen, and learn.

MADE FOR TODDLERS (or people who act like toddlers)
• 24 friendly animals, from the farm (cow, pig, sheep, duck, horse) to the wild (lion, tiger, wolf, monkey) and beyond (owl, frog, cicada, peacock)
• Name first, then the sound. Every animal gets its name spoken, followed by one of several real recordings, so it stays fresh on every tap.
• Between 3 and 10 sounds per animal
• Big, bright pictures in two wide columns on iPhone, more on iPad. Easy for little hands to tap, hard to break.

SAFE BY DESIGN
• 100% offline. No internet connection required, ever. The app makes no network requests at all.
• Each sound verified individually by a human for quality and content
• No ads, no pop-ups, no in-app purchases
• No tracking, no analytics, no data collection
• No accounts and no sign-ups

MAKE IT YOUR OWN
• Hidden settings area for parents, just draw a square with your finger.
• Adjust the voice's speed and pitch.
• Pick from the voices available on your device.
• Turn the spoken animal names on or off.

Animal Spin is open source, and its sounds and images come from Creative Commons and public-domain sources.

Perfect for parent-child joint play time, car rides, or a few minutes of learning. Download once and it works anywhere.
